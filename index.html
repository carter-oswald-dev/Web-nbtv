Black and white formats use vertical lines scanned bottom to top, right to left. A blacker-than-black pulse ends every line except the last, and the missing pulse marks a new frame. NBSC adds an NTSC-style colour subcarrier, with a reference burst filling the first line of each frame. CCNC colour isn't supported; those files play here in black and white on the 32-line standard.

Microphone input works best with a cable from the sending device's headphone socket.
